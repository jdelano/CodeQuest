Pipeworks: Pro Tools Signal Flow

Connect the pipes to build a clean, correct signal path.

Goal

Connections: 0 / 8 correct Build the chain

Click two nodes to connect them. Click a connected pipe to remove it. Press R to reset.

Key Terms

Insert Processor placed directly in the channel strip (EQ, compressor).
Send Copies signal to an Aux/Bus without interrupting the main path.
Bus Shared internal path that routes multiple tracks to a destination.
Gain Staging Keeping levels healthy from input to output to avoid clipping.
ADC/DAC Converts analog to digital and digital back to analog.
Fader Controls channel output level after inserts and EQ.

Candy Trading Game

Students evaluate their endowment, trade, then reevaluate.

Setup

Click a bar on one student, then click a bar on another student to swap. Trades only make sense if both students value what they receive more.

Key Ideas

Subjective Value Each student rates each candy type from 1–10.
Gains from Trade Trades can make both parties better off.
Endowment Effect People may overvalue what they already own.
Reevaluation After trading, students reassess total value.

Classroom

Round 0: 0 trades
